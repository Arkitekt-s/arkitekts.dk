PROJECT 04 — INTEGRATION & AUTOMATION

Basecamp Response Monitor.

Helping teams respond to customer messages before they get missed.

At TwentyFour, I worked on a monitoring application that checked Basecamp project conversations and sent Slack alerts when a customer message needed a response.

01 / OVERVIEW+

THE PROJECT

Understand the challenge.

A team working across many client projects can miss a conversation. The goal was to identify messages waiting for replies and notify the right people without constant manual checking.

THE MAIN FOCUS

A useful, event-driven connection between customer messages and internal team alerts.

02 / EXECUTION+

HOW I APPROACHED IT

From idea to execution.

  1. 01

    Monitor conversations

    Tracked activity across multiple Basecamp projects and customer contexts.

  2. 02

    Automate follow-up

    Used API integration and webhooks to route relevant unanswered-message alerts into Slack.

  3. 03

    Deliver securely

    Worked with token-based authentication, two-factor authentication, a Node.js backend and CI/CD practices.

TOOLS & TECHNOLOGIES

  • TypeScript
  • Slack Bolt
  • REST APIs

What the work delivered.

The original case study describes delivery through two Scrum phases, from July 2023 to January 2024. The goal was timely follow-up, with clearer customer communication and fewer missed interactions.
